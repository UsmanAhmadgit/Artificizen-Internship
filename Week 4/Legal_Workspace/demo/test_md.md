## Executive Summary

This internal compliance audit was conducted to evaluate whether City Builders Ltd. followed contractual obligations, workplace safety regulations, procurement policies, and internal governance standards during the construction of multiple commercial projects. The audit reviewed project documentation, procurement records, employee interviews, financial approvals, and site inspection reports. While the company demonstrated satisfactory record keeping in several administrative areas, auditors identified significant weaknesses in procurement approval procedures, document retention practices, and safety compliance monitoring. Immediate corrective action was recommended to reduce legal and operational risks.

---

## Audit Findings

### Finding 1 – Procurement Policy

The audit determined that purchase orders exceeding PKR 2,000,000 were approved without obtaining the required secondary management authorization. Four procurement requests were processed using emergency approval procedures despite no documented emergency conditions. Auditors recommended implementing an automated approval workflow to prevent unauthorized purchases.

### Finding 2 – Safety Compliance

Monthly safety inspections were not consistently documented. Several inspection reports were missing supervisor signatures, and training records for newly hired construction workers were incomplete. Personal protective equipment inventory records were also outdated.

### Finding 3 – Document Retention

Project correspondence, engineering change requests, and supplier invoices were stored across multiple systems without a standardized retention policy. Auditors concluded that the absence of centralized document management increased the risk of incomplete legal evidence during litigation.

---

## Risk Assessment

| Risk Area | Risk Level | Recommendation |
|-----------|------------|----------------|
| Procurement Approval | High | Require dual approval |
| Safety Documentation | Medium | Monthly compliance review |
| Document Retention | High | Centralized document repository |
| Employee Training | Medium | Quarterly refresher sessions |
| Vendor Management | Low | Annual vendor evaluation |

---

## Required Corrective Actions

1. Implement a digital procurement approval workflow.
2. Archive all legal documents within a centralized repository.
3. Schedule mandatory quarterly safety training.
4. Review document retention policies every six months.
5. Assign compliance ownership to department managers.

---

## Auditor Comments

> The majority of identified compliance issues were procedural rather than intentional misconduct. Strengthening internal controls will significantly reduce future legal exposure and improve regulatory compliance.
